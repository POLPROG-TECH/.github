## Who we are

**POLPROG** is a Polish technology company building custom software as well as its own applications, platforms and tools.

We take projects from concept and architecture, through implementation and testing, to deployment and ongoing development. We focus on clean code, security, performance and accessibility. We choose technologies to fit the product, avoid unnecessary complexity and design solutions that can be developed and maintained for years.
